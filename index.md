I'm a data engineer, energy nerd, and founder of a small (but mighty!) [tennis brand](https://bageled.nyc/). I've built data pipelines, managed software teams, read [over a dozen books](https://www.goodreads.com/list/show/414443.History_of_energy) on the history of energy (my neice still makes fun of me for reading about the [history of coal mining in Europe](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/204557935-the-age-of-coal)), led fundraising for a [local non-profit](https://www.fortgreenetennis.org/), and designed products that found their way [into New York Magazine](https://bageled.nyc/Press). 

By day, I'm embedded within the energy industry as a software engineer. At nights and on weekends, I continue to build out the aforementioned tennis brand. You could say that I'm a firm believer in liking my work and having fun side projects.
